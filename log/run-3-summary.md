# Run 3: Cartesia, Sonic 3.6, two stock voices

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | 11 October 2026, 07:45 to 07:50 UTC |
| Tool and plan | Cartesia, Free plan |
| Model | Sonic 3.6 |
| Voices | Daniel - Modern Assistant for lines 01 to 06, Rubel - City Guide for lines 07 to 12 |
| Settings | Speed 1.0x, volume 1.0x, no emotion chosen, language set to Auto-detect, 44.1 kHz |
| Script | `scripts/script-pack-v1.csv`, 12 lines, 1,141 characters |
| Generated and judged by | Muntasir Ahmad Chowdhury |

## Why two voices

The rules ask for one voice for all 12 lines. Cartesia's voice picker, filtered to Bengali, listed five voices, and none of the English voices was among them. So the six English lines used one English voice and the six mixed lines used one Bengali voice, as rule 1 in the README allows.

## Result

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Lines accepted on the first take | 12 of 12 |
| Lines that needed a retake | 0 of 12 (0%) |
| Lines that failed after three takes | 0 |
| Accepted audio | 85.28 seconds (1.42 minutes) |
| Credit balance before | 20,000 remaining |
| Credit balance after | 18,647 remaining |
| Credits used | 1,353 |

All six English lines and all six mixed Bengali and English lines were judged correct on the first take. No words, prices, dates or brand names were logged as wrong.

## Two extra generations

The history shows 14 generations, not 12. Lines 04 and 12 were each generated twice within the same minute. The founder reported both lines correct on take 1, and the reason for the second generation was not recorded. The log keeps both extra generations as take 2, marked "not judged". They cost 212 credits, and the figures below include them, because the README counts every credit used in a run.

## Cost per finished minute

Nothing was paid, because the run used the Free plan. Worked out at the regular price of the cheapest paid plan on the test date (Pro, $5 a month for 100,000 credits, which is $0.00005 a credit):

- 1,353 credits is $0.07 for 1.42 minutes of accepted audio, or about **$0.05 per finished minute**.
- That is about 952 credits per finished minute.
- Without the two extra generations it would be 1,141 credits, about $0.04 per finished minute.

## How the figures were read

- Credits: the Subscription page showed "Model Credits Remaining" as 20,000 before the run and 18,647 after. The same page prices text to speech at 1 credit per character, and the 14 generations add up to exactly 1,353 characters.
- Clip lengths: the history does not show a length. Each entry was played once in the browser with the sound off, and the lengths of the audio pieces the app streamed were added up. The figures are good to about a tenth of a second.
- Times: the history shows local time to the minute. The log gives the same times in UTC.

## Limits of this run

- Two voices, one model and twelve short lines.
- One listener judged every take.
- The audio was not downloaded. Cartesia's pricing page lists a commercial licence from the Pro plan up.
