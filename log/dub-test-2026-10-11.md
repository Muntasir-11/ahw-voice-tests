## Why v1 and why Hindi

- The Free plan's monthly credits cover 0.4 minutes of Dubbing v2 or 5 minutes of Dubbing v1, according to the pricing page on 10 October 2026. A one-minute clip only fits on v1.
- Bengali was not offered as a target language in either model's menu, so the test could not dub into Bengali. Hindi is a language the founder can judge.

## Credits

| | |
|---|---|
| Quote shown beside the start button, with the Watermark option on | 2,209 credits |
| Credits used, as shown in the finished dub's details | 3,311.93 |
| "Watermarked" in the finished dub's details | No |
| Credits used on the account before | 1,026 of 10,000 |
| Credits used on the account after | 4,338 of 10,000 |

The quote and the charge differ. 2,209 credits is the watermarked rate of 2,000 credits a minute for a 66-second clip. 3,312 credits is the rate without a watermark, 3,000 credits a minute. The finished dub is recorded as not watermarked. The records do not show whether the Watermark option was switched off between the screenshot and the start, so we report both numbers and draw no conclusion about why they differ.

At the Creator plan's credit price on the test date ($22 for 121,000 credits), 3,312 credits is about $0.60, or about $0.55 for each dubbed minute.

## What was not tested

- Dubbing v2, the default model. The Free allowance was too small for this clip.
- Dubbing Studio editing. The finished dub shows an "Edit in Studio" button, and it was not pressed.
- More than one speaker, background music, or a clip longer than about a minute.
- Lip sync, which ElevenLabs' documentation does not list as a dubbing feature.
