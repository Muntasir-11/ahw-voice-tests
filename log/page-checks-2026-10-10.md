## Resemble AI

The page `resemble.ai/products/voice-creation` redirected to `resemble.ai/product-notice`. The notice is headed "This product is no longer available". It says the company has stopped offering voice generation as a commercial product and now works on AI detection, watermarking and security, and that the voice models it built remain open and free for research and development.

The Resemble pricing page listed detection plans only: Flex at $0 a month, Team at $280 a month and Business at $800 a month, the last two billed annually.
