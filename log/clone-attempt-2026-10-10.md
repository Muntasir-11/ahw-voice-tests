# Instant Voice Clone attempt on the Free plan

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | 10 October 2026 |
| Tool and plan | ElevenLabs, Free plan |
| Feature | Instant Voice Clone |
| Voice | The founder's own voice, recorded for this attempt |
| Outcome | No clone was created. The app asked for an upgrade at the save step. |

## What happened

1. The Create voice window listed Instant Voice Clone with no lock. Professional Voice Clone was locked, with a note that it needs the Creator plan or higher. The window showed 0 of 3 voices created.
2. The upload screen asked for at least 10 seconds of audio, as files of up to 10 MB each or a recording made in the browser.
3. The founder supplied a recording of his own voice, named the voice, chose a language label and ticked the consent statement.
4. Pressing "Save voice" opened a window titled "Upgrade to use your cloned voice". It offered Starter at $1 for the first month and $6 a month after that, or $5 a month billed annually.

No subscription was bought, so no clone exists and no clone audio was generated or judged.
